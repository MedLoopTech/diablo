# Diabetes Remission — Medical Philosophy (Domain Extensions)

Extends [CONSTITUTION.md](../../CONSTITUTION.md) §2. Domain-specific guidance for content agents.

## Remission criteria (content reference)

- **Primary:** HbA1c < 6.5% sustained without increasing diabetes medication
- **Supporting markers:** Fasting glucose normalization, weight loss (especially visceral), improved insulin sensitivity
- **Timeline:** Meaningful change often visible by day 30–45; remission criteria evaluated at day 90 and follow-up

Always frame as achievable for *many* people, not everyone. Individual results depend on duration of diabetes, medication type, adherence, and medical supervision.

## Three-phase program (content detail)

### Phase 1: Baseline (Days 1–14)

**Content themes:** Habit formation, logging glucose and meals, understanding personal patterns, no drastic restriction yet.

**Patient message:** "We're learning how *your* body responds — not following a generic diet sheet."

**Key actions:** Log glucose 1–2× daily, log one meal with photo, 10-minute post-dinner walk.

### Phase 2: Intervention (Days 15–60)

**Content themes:** Plate method, smart swaps, post-meal movement, reducing high-GL meals, building fasting window gradually.

**Patient message:** "Small swaps in your regular kitchen — not a new cuisine."

**Key actions:** ½–¼–¼ plate at lunch and dinner, swap white rice portion 2–3× weekly, 15-minute post-meal walk.

### Phase 3: Stabilization (Days 61–90)

**Content themes:** Sustaining changes, social eating (weddings, Eid), travel, stress management, preparing for alumni program.

**Patient message:** "This is the rest of your life, not a 90-day crash diet."

## Smart swaps (reference list for writers)

| Instead of | Try | Why |
|------------|-----|-----|
| White rice (large portion) | Half portion + extra daal/sabzi | Lower glycemic load |
| Maida paratha daily | Whole-wheat roti or besan cheela | Lower GI, more protein |
| Sweet chai 3× daily | One sweet, rest without sugar | Cumulative glucose spike |
| Fried pakora snack | Roasted chana or peanuts (small handful) | Protein + fat blunts spike |
| Late-night naan + curry | Earlier dinner + post-meal walk | Timing + movement |
| Mithai at every occasion | Small portion + walk after | Portion + activity |

## Fasting guidance (content-safe)

- **General:** Many patients benefit from compressing eating window (e.g. 12:12 → 14:10 → 16:8 over weeks).
- **Mandatory caveat:** Anyone on insulin, sulfonylureas (glimepiride, gliclazide), or with history of hypoglycemia must discuss fasting with their doctor before starting.
- **Never:** Prescribe a specific fasting protocol as medical advice in content.

## Medication content boundary

Content may **educate** that remission sometimes allows medication reduction **under doctor supervision**.

Content must **never**:
- Suggest stopping, starting, or changing doses
- Imply patient can adjust meds based on glucose readings alone
- Compare drug efficacy (pharma territory — route to medical team)

| Source | Use for |

Cite with evidence level. Do not cherry-pick single studies for miracle framing.
