# Diabetes Remission — Topic Pillars

Ranked content pillars for editorial calendar and SEO planning. ★ = priority tier.

## Tier 1 (★★★★★) — Core program

### Pillar 1: Diabetes Remission (90-day program)
- What remission means (and what it doesn't)
- Who is a candidate / who should talk to doctor first
- Day-by-day journey overview (3 phases)
- Care pod model explained
- Success stories (with disclaimers, no guaranteed outcomes)
- Cohort community and accountability

### Pillar 2: Desi Nutrition / Plate Method
- ½–¼–¼ plate rule with Pakistani meals
- GI vs GL explained simply
- Smart swaps (roti, rice, daal, biryani, chai)
- Eating out: dhaba, wedding, iftar
- Ramadan / Eid special guidance (with doctor caveat)
- Meal timing and post-meal spikes

### Pillar 3: Insulin Resistance & Weight Loss
- What insulin resistance means in plain language
- Visceral fat and liver fat (without fear-mongering)
- Weight loss as remission pathway (DiRECT framing)
- Plateaus and how to handle them
- Muscle preservation during weight loss

### Pillar 4: Post-Meal Movement & Home Exercise
- 10–15 minute post-meal walk science
- Home yoga routines (no gym)
- Bodyweight strength for insulin sensitivity
- Movement when fasting or in Ramadan
- Coach-led vs self-guided content

### Pillar 5: Glucose Monitoring & Patterns
- How to log glucose (meter photo, timing)
- Fasting vs post-meal readings
- Reading patterns, not single numbers
- When to call doctor (250/70/180 thresholds)
- Understanding HbA1c vs daily glucose

## Tier 2 (★★★★) — Supporting topics

### Pillar 6: Fasting
- Intermittent fasting basics
- Building window gradually
- Safety with medications (mandatory doctor caveat)
- Fasting during Ramadan (specialized content)

### Pillar 7: Sleep & Stress
- Sleep and morning glucose
- Cortisol and cravings
- Family stress and food habits
- Simple stress tools (walk, breathing — not medical treatment)

### Pillar 8: Medication (education only)
- What metformin does (general education)
- Why doctor adjusts meds, not patient
- GLP-1 context for pharma audience only
- Medication audit trail (doctor audience)

### Pillar 9: CGM & Labs
- When CGM helps
- HbA1c, fasting glucose, lipid panel basics
- Lab prep (Chughtai, Aga Khan, SKM)
- Interpreting trends with doctor

## Tier 3 (★★★) — Engagement & conversion

### Pillar 10: Recipes & Meal Plans
- 7-day desi meal plans
- High-protein breakfast ideas
- Low-GL lunch boxes for office
- Snack alternatives

### Pillar 11: Care Pod & Doctor Partnership
- How to join via doctor referral
- Doctor dashboard benefits (B2B)
- Nutritionist and coach roles
- Referral program
