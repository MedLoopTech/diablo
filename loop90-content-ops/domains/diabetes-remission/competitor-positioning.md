# Competitor Positioning — Diabetes Remission

Expands `CONSTITUTION.md` §9. **Admire, don't copy.** Never name a competitor disparagingly in published content.

## Positioning statement

> Loop/90 is Virta/DiRECT-grade remission evidence, applied through a Pakistani kitchen, delivered by a doctor-led care pod, with AI handling the daily layer.

Four claims, all defensible:

| Claim | Why it holds |
|-------|--------------|
| **Evidence-based** | DiRECT trial, Roy Taylor's mechanism, remission defined as HbA1c < 6.5% without increasing medication |
| **Desi-native** | Roti, daal, biryani, chai as the default context — GI/GL reasoning on real Pakistani meals |
| **Doctor-led** | One doctor, one nutritionist, one movement coach per cohort — not an app with a chatbot |
| **Cohort economics** | One care pod serves 30–50 people, which is what makes it affordable here |

## We are NOT

- A US telehealth clone
- A supplement or MLM play
- A generic wellness / step-counter app
- An AI that replaces doctors
- A keto-evangelist brand

## Reference set

### Virta Health (US)
- **What they got right:** rigorous outcome publishing, remission as the headline metric, continuous remote care model
- **Where we differ:** their food model is US-keto and their price point is US-insurance; ours is desi-food and cohort-priced for Pakistan
- **Borrow:** outcome transparency, "remission not management" framing
- **Don't borrow:** aggressive keto positioning, US clinical-trial tone

### DiRECT Trial (UK, academic)
- **What it is:** the landmark remission RCT — the evidence spine of our whole proposition
- **How we use it:** cite for the remission definition and the weight-loss pathway. Evidence level A.
- **Care:** it's a *study*, not a competitor. Never imply affiliation or endorsement.

### Diet Doctor (Sweden/global)
- **What they got right:** genuinely excellent free educational content, clean visual recipe format
- **Where we differ:** they're content-only, no care team, no monitoring; and Western food throughout
- **Borrow:** clarity of food explainers, visual plate formats
- **Don't borrow:** low-carb-as-ideology framing

### Levels Health (US)
- **What they got right:** making CGM data legible and motivating to non-diabetics
- **Where we differ:** CGM is a Tier-2 topic for us, not the product; our market is largely meter-based
- **Borrow:** pattern-over-single-reading education
- **Don't borrow:** quantified-self aesthetic, biohacker vocabulary

### Peter Attia / Ben Bikman / Eric Westman (clinician-educators)
- **What they got right:** mechanism-first explanation that respects the reader's intelligence
- **How we use it:** informs our insulin-resistance pillar for Persona 2 (who wants mechanism)
- **Care:** these are individuals with their own brands — cite ideas, never imply endorsement, never name-drop for credibility

## Local / regional landscape

| Category | Reality | Our angle |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Hospital diabetes clinics | Strong clinically, no between-visit monitoring | We fill the 3–6 month gap, not replace the clinic |
| Standalone nutritionists | Skilled but 1:1 and expensive | We give them cohort income; we're a channel, not a competitor |
| Generic health apps | Step counters, no clinical layer, no local food | Doctor-led + desi food + escalation thresholds |
| Herbal / hakeem remedies | Culturally trusted, evidence-free, sometimes harmful | Never mock. Redirect to evidence gently; never claim they "cure" anything either |
| WhatsApp diabetes groups | Where patients already are | Meet them there — WhatsApp is our top channel, not a rival |

## Comparison rules for content

**Allowed:**
- Compare *approaches* generically ("some programs use elimination diets; we use portion geometry")
- Cite DiRECT and published research by name
- State what we don't do

**Not allowed:**
- Named negative comparison ("unlike [Brand], we…")
- Claiming superiority without published evidence
- Implying partnership, affiliation, or endorsement by any referenced brand or clinician
- Mocking traditional/local remedies or the people who use them

## Differentiation one-liners (approved)

- "A care pod, not a chatbot."
- "Built for roti, not quinoa."
- "Remission is a documented outcome — we won't promise it's yours."
- "The AI handles your daily questions. Your doctor handles your medication. Always."
