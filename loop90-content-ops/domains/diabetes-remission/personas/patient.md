# Persona 1 — Patient (Type 2 Diabetes)

Primary audience. Most content defaults to this persona unless the brief says otherwise.

## Profile

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Age | 38–58 |
| Location | Karachi, Lahore, Islamabad (urban / peri-urban) |
| Diagnosis | T2D, 1–10 years ago |
| Typical HbA1c | 7.5–9.5% |
| Medication | Metformin, often a second agent |
| Household | Married, 2–4 children, shared family meals |
| Device | Android phone, WhatsApp-first, limited data plan |
| English | Reads simple English; prefers Urdu for emotional content |

## What they actually want

1. To stop feeling like diabetes is a life sentence that only gets worse
2. To eat with their family without being the person eating something separate
3. To not be scolded at every doctor visit
4. To get off medication — or at least not add more
5. Straight answers about roti, rice, and chai — not "eat healthy"

## Emotional state

Tired of vague advice. Has been told "avoid sugar" for years with no plan. Often carries guilt from a previous failed attempt. Fatalistic streak: *"Yeh to hota hi hai, sab ko hota hai"* (everyone gets it). Responds strongly to hope backed by a concrete plan, and shuts down at fear or shame.

## Objections to pre-empt

| Objection | Response angle |
|-----------|----------------|
| "Diabetes can't be reversed." | Remission is documented (DiRECT). Explain what remission means — HbA1c under 6.5% without increasing medication — and who it works best for. |
| "I can't give up roti and rice." | You don't. Portion geometry and smart swaps, not elimination. Show the plate. |
| "I don't have time to exercise." | 10–15 minute post-meal walk. No gym. |
| "This is expensive." | Cohort model shares one care pod across 30–50 people. Say what's included. |
| "I've tried before and failed." | Consistency beats perfection. Never guilt a slip. Restart today. |
| "Is this just an app?" | No — a real doctor, nutritionist, and coach, with AI handling the daily layer. |

## Voice cues

- Simple, conversational, grade 6–8
- Warm greeting where natural: Salaam / Assalam o Alaikum
- Desi food examples always (roti, daal, biryani, chai, kachumber)
- Code-switching acceptable: *"thoda sa"*, *"bilkul"*, *"aaj se shuru"*
- Second person, present tense, one action per piece
- Never lecture. One or two sentences of science, then what to do about it.

## Channels (priority)

WhatsApp ★★★★★ · In-app ★★★★★ · Email ★★★★ · Short video ★★★★ · Blog ★★★

## Content that works

- "What one roti actually does to your glucose" (GI/GL, concrete)
- "The 10-minute walk that changes your after-dinner number"
- Day-in-the-life of a cohort member
- Ramadan and Eid survival guides
- Plate method with a real Pakistani thali photo

## Content that fails

- Western meal plans (quinoa, kale, overnight oats)
- Long science essays without a takeaway
- Anything implying they caused this and should feel bad
- Guaranteed outcomes or before/after HbA1c promises

## Safety notes

- Any medication question → route to their pod doctor, never answer directly
- Glucose ≥ 250 or ≤ 70 → urgent guidance, contact doctor immediately
- Fasting content must carry the insulin/sulfonylurea caveat
- Disclaimer block on all medical content
