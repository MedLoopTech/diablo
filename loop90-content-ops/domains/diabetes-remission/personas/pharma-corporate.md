# Persona 5 — Pharma / Corporate Partner (B2B2C)

Enterprise partnership audience. Longest sales cycle, highest contract value, most compliance scrutiny. Source of truth for hooks and framing: `products/pitches/PITCHES.md` Pitch 3.

## Profile

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Role | Brand manager, medical affairs lead, market access, country manager |
| Company | Multinational or local pharma with a diabetes portfolio in Pakistan |
| Products | GLP-1s (Ozempic, Victoza), DPP-4s (Galvus), orals, insulins |
| Metric they own | Prescription volume, refill/adherence rate, market share |
| Setting | Conference meeting or formal sales call, 10-minute slot |
| Reads | Executive summary + data tables — not narrative |

## Their problem (state it as theirs, not ours)

An **adherence crisis**, not an awareness problem.

- Patient starts therapy, it works, then they stop — cost, side effects, no visible feedback, no follow-up
- Oral diabetes medication adherence in Pakistan is roughly **40–60% at 6 months**
- Roughly **40% discontinue GLP-1 therapy within 6 months**
- Every drop-off is lost recurring revenue *and* a missed health outcome

They already have doctor relationships and brand awareness. What they lack is a channel to the patient **between prescriptions**.

## What a partnership looks like

1. Prescribing doctor refers the patient via a co-branded QR / WhatsApp link
2. Patient logs glucose, meals, weight; gets AI coaching and reminders
3. Doctor sees the trend dashboard — including whether the patient is logging, a proxy for adherence
4. Partner receives **anonymised, aggregated** adherence data: % still logging at 30/60/90 days, average glucose improvement, drop-off points

**They pay for:** sponsored patient slots, branded onboarding, monthly aggregate adherence report.
**They get:** better retention, real-world evidence for market access, doctor goodwill, presence at point of care.

## The ask

Pilot with 3–5 doctors in one city. Co-branded patient experience. 90 days → adherence report for them, case study for us. **No exclusivity at pilot stage.**

## Objections to pre-empt

| Objection | Response angle |
|-----------|----------------|
| "Is this a promotional channel for our drug?" | No. Loop/90 never recommends or names a medication to patients. Co-branding is on the program wrapper, not clinical content. |
| "What about patient data / privacy?" | Aggregate and anonymised only — never individual patient data. PDPB consent built in. |
| "Is this a regulated medical device?" | No — practice management and patient engagement software. Clinical decisions stay with the physician. |
| "How is this different from a wellness app?" | Doctor and nutritionist in the loop, medication audit trail, escalation alerts, built for Pakistan (desi food, Urdu, phone-first). |
| "Can you prove the adherence effect?" | That's what the pilot measures. Don't claim the outcome before the data exists. |

## Voice cues

- Executive summary first, then data tables — never a story-led open
- Their metrics, their language: adherence, persistence, real-world evidence, market access
- Compliance-forward — lead with what the AI *won't* do
- No patient-facing warmth, no motivational framing
- Concise; assume a 10-minute slot and a skim

## Channels (priority)

Direct meeting / formal pitch ★★★★★ · LinkedIn ★★★★ · Conference material ★★★★ · Email (executive summary) ★★★★

## Content that works

- Adherence economics with the 40–60% figure sourced
- Compliance architecture explainer (what AI never touches)
- Pilot structure with a defined 90-day deliverable
- Real-world evidence positioning for market access teams

## Content that fails

- Patient testimonials as primary evidence
- Anything implying Loop/90 influences prescribing choice
- Outcome claims not yet backed by pilot data
- Consumer wellness aesthetics or motivational copy

## Safety and compliance notes

- **Never** position Loop/90 as driving drug selection or as promotional material aimed at prescribing
- Only aggregate, anonymised data leaves the platform — say this explicitly in every pharma asset
- All statistics must be sourced; "estimated at 40–60%" must keep its hedge
- GLP-1 and drug-class content is for **this persona only** — never patient-facing (per `topic-pillars.md` Pillar 8)
