# Persona 3 — Doctor / GP / Endocrinologist

B2B decision-maker. Buys the clinic subscription and/or leads a care pod. Source of truth for hooks and objections: `products/pitches/PITCHES.md` Pitch 1.

## Profile

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Role | GP, consultant physician, or endocrinologist |
| Setting | Private clinic or hospital OPD, Karachi / Lahore / Islamabad |
| Panel | 50–200 diabetic patients |
| Visit cadence | Patients seen every 3–6 months |
| Current tools | WhatsApp groups, paper notes, clinic billing software |
| Time per patient | 7–12 minutes |
| Device | iPhone or Android; desktop in clinic |

## The pain (in their words)

Not *treating* diabetes — **monitoring it between visits**.

- Three to six months pass between appointments and a lot goes wrong in that gap
- Deteriorating patients don't call; they wait until it's bad
- No glucose history at the visit — just what the patient remembers
- Medication changes happen verbally, unlogged, with no audit trail
- Delegating follow-up to an assistant means exposing confidential records

## What they want

1. To see who's in trouble *before* they call
2. An audit trail on every medication change
3. Fewer routine calls, more time on patients who need intervention
4. To not be replaced or second-guessed by software
5. Revenue that doesn't cost them clinic hours

## Objections to pre-empt

| Objection | Response angle |
|-----------|----------------|
| "My patients don't use apps." | They use WhatsApp. Invite is a WhatsApp link → phone number → done. |
| "I already use WhatsApp / paper." | This doesn't replace conversation. It fills the gap where nothing is recorded — readings, med changes, lost questions. |
| "Is this DRAP-approved?" | Practice management software, not a medical device — same category as clinic billing. All clinical decisions remain theirs. |
| "What if the AI gives wrong advice?" | AI answers routine lifestyle questions only. Anything clinical routes to them with a flag and full context. AI never touches medication. |
| "I don't have time to learn a system." | 30-day free trial, we onboard the first 10 patients on the first call. No contract. |
| "Will this replace me?" | Explicitly no. AI scales the daily layer; judgment stays human. Say this plainly. |

## Commercial facts (keep accurate)

- **PKR 4,500/month** for 1 doctor + 50 patients — less than one consultation fee
- 30-day free trial, no contract

## Voice cues

- Professional medical writing — no patient-style warmth
- ROI-forward and specific: time saved, alerts caught, audit trail
- Respect their expertise; never explain diabetes to a diabetologist
- Lead with the monitoring gap, not with technology
- Short. They read on a phone between patients.

## Content that works

- The 3-month monitoring gap, quantified
- Audit trail and medico-legal protection angle

## Content that fails

- Overselling AI capability

- Consumer wellness aesthetics

## Safety and terminology notes

- **"Reverse" and "guaranteed" are banned in doctor-facing content** — remission only, with the DiRECT definition
- Never imply the platform diagnoses or prescribes
- "Clinical decisions remain yours" is the required disclaimer for this channel
