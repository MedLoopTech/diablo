# Persona 4 — Nutritionist & Movement Coach

Care pod recruitment audience. Loop/90 needs one nutritionist and one movement coach per cohort of 30–50 patients.

## Profile

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Role | Clinical nutritionist / dietitian, or yoga / fitness coach |
| Age | 25–40 |
| Setting | Freelance, small studio, or part-time clinic attachment |
| Income | Irregular — per-session, high churn, constant client acquisition |
| Clients | 5–25 active, mostly one-to-one |
| Device | Instagram-heavy, phone-first |
| Credential | BS Nutrition / registered dietitian, or a yoga/fitness certification |

## What they actually want

1. **Predictable income** instead of chasing individual clients
2. Clients who are already committed and pre-screened
3. To not do their own marketing
4. Professional legitimacy — working alongside a doctor, not competing with one
5. To help more people without burning out on 1:1 sessions

## Emotional state

Skilled but under-utilized. Frustrated that "diet advice" is treated as free. Wary of platforms that take a big cut or dump unqualified leads on them. Proud of their expertise and sensitive to being positioned as an app feature.

## Objections to pre-empt

| Objection | Response angle |
|-----------|----------------|
| "Will AI replace me?" | AI handles routine daily Q&A so their time goes to the work that actually needs a human. Say what the AI does *not* do. |
| "How much do I earn?" | Be concrete about the cohort model and payout. Never vague. |
| "How many hours?" | Cohort-based, not per-client — group reviews plus escalations. Give a real number. |
| "Are these serious clients?" | Patients are enrolled in a paid 90-day program with a doctor. Pre-committed by design. |
| "Do I need to find my own clients?" | No. Cohort assignment comes from the platform. |
| "Am I liable for medical outcomes?" | The pod doctor owns clinical decisions; they own nutrition/movement guidance within scope. |

## Voice cues

- Peer-to-peer professional, warmer than doctor content
- Respect the credential — never "anyone can do this"
- Concrete on money, time, and scope
- Growth and impact framing, not just income
- Instagram-native for top of funnel; longer-form on landing

## Channels (priority)

Instagram ★★★★★ · LinkedIn ★★★★ · WhatsApp ★★★★ · Professional landing page ★★★★ · Referral from existing pod staff ★★★★★

## Content that works

- "What a care pod nutritionist actually does in a week"
- Income and hours, stated plainly
- Scope-of-practice clarity (what's yours, what's the doctor's)
- Existing pod staff testimonial
- The AI-handles-the-repetitive-stuff pitch

## Content that fails

- Anything implying they're interchangeable or automatable
- Vague "join our platform" with no numbers
- Patient-facing tone
- Overpromising client volume

## Safety and scope notes

- Nutritionists and coaches **never** advise on medication — that routes to the pod doctor
- Content for this persona should reinforce the escalation thresholds (≥250 / ≤70 / ≥180) as *their* triggers to escalate
- Referral payouts to pod staff exist — keep amounts accurate per the platform's current settings, never invented
