# Research Memo Template

Output format for the `medical-research` skill. One memo per content brief, consumed by `writing` and re-checked by `medical-safety`.

Copy everything below the line.

---

```markdown

**Brief:** [link or ID]
**Domain:** diabetes-remission
**Persona:** [patient | prediabetic-family | doctor | nutritionist-coach | pharma-corporate]
```

---

## Rules

- **2–5 references.** Fewer means under-researched; more means the piece is too broad — split it.
- **Every number gets a row** in the numbers table with its exact approved wording. This is what stops a writer from rounding 86% to "almost everyone".
- **Counter-evidence is mandatory.** An article that only presents supporting evidence fails rubric A1.
- **Hedges are set here, not in editing.** If the memo says "associated with", the draft says "associated with".
