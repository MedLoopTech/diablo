# Playbook — Growth Marketer

For whoever owns acquisition. This system will support aggressive growth and refuse dishonest growth. Knowing where the line sits saves you cycles.

---

## What you can say

| Claim | Where |
|-------|-------|
| "Remission" — defined properly | Everywhere |
| "Reverse" / "reversal" | **B2C landing and ads only**, with remission defined on the same page and legal terms in the footer |
| "Money-back guarantee" | B2C landing legal section — attached to the refund, never to a health outcome |
| Care pod, 90 days, cohorts of 30–50 | Everywhere — stable facts |
| DiRECT evidence, cited properly | Everywhere |
| "A real shot at remission" | Everywhere — it promises nothing |

## What you cannot say, anywhere

- Cure, cured, miracle, 100% effective
- "Guaranteed results" (guarantee must attach to the refund, not the outcome)
- Any specific outcome number we haven't published
- Anything about medication reduction as a program result
- Named negative comparison to a competitor

---

## The four conditions for "reverse"

All four, or it's blocked:

1. Declared in the brief's `banned_term_exceptions`
2. Channel is B2C landing or ads
3. Remission is defined **on the same page**
4. The legal/disclaimer block is on the page

The most common block is #3 — the H1 says "reverse", and remission is never defined anywhere. Ten-minute fix.

---

## Testimonials

| Element | Rule |
|---------|------|
| Subjective ("this changed my life") | Fine |
| Specific HbA1c or weight numbers | Requires "individual results vary" **adjacent**, not in a footer |
| "I came off my medication" | Blocked — medication outcome, even as a quote |
| Before/after imagery | Blocked if it implies a typical result |
| Consent | Verified, always |

---

## Keywords vs claims

Some of the highest-intent queries in this niche are terms we restrict. You can target the **intent** without the term.

| Query | Approach |
|-------|----------|
| "reverse diabetes" | Target the underlying question — "is remission possible?" — and answer honestly. High-intent, defensible, and better content. |
| "cure diabetes" | Never targeted. Not in title, H1, meta, slug, or hashtag. |

`seo` will reject a keyword that forces a banned claim. That's not obstruction — a page that ranks for a claim we can't defend is a liability, not an asset.

---

## Facts that will get you held at publish

These change and cannot be recalled:

- Pricing, any tier
- Currency (admin-configurable — never hardcode PKR in copy)
- Referral payout amounts (per-code and variable — there is no single figure)
- Which features ship today

Have the current numbers ready when you brief a landing page, or expect a hold.
