Scoring instruments used by the `editing` and `medical-safety` skills.

| File | Purpose | Gate |
|------|---------|------|
| [medical-article-rubric.md](medical-article-rubric.md) | 100-point score across evidence, safety, voice, clarity, actionability | ≥ 85/100 to publish (≥ 64/75 non-medical) |
