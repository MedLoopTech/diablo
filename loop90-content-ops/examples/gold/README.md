## The two lessons so far

**From the landing FAQ:** the most persuasive passage in the corpus is the one that concedes the most. *"It's not a cure"* on a sales page is the differentiator, not a compliance tax.

**From the plate rule:** specificity is a safety feature. "Eat more vegetables" gets interpreted; "kachumber, bhindi, karela" gets cooked.
