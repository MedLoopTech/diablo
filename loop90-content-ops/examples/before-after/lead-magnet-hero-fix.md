# Before / After — Lead Magnet Hero

A real compliance failure from `desi_diabetes_plate_guide_bilingual.html`, and the fix. See [../gold/source-audit.md](../gold/source-audit.md).

---

## Before

> **The Desi Diabetes Plate Guide**
> No Quinoa Bowls Required.
> **Reverse Type 2 Diabetes** & Lower HbA1c using everyday Pakistani kitchen foods.
>
> […]
>
> **Ready to Reverse Diabetes in 90 Days?**
> ✓ Lab-verified HbA1c Tests ✓ **Guarantee Included** ✓ 100% Desi Kitchen Friendly

### Why it's blocked

| Issue | Rule |
|-------|------|
| "Reverse" ×4 on a lead magnet | Permitted on B2C landing/ads only |
| Remission never defined anywhere in the guide | Condition 3 of the four-condition test |
| "Guarantee Included" attached to nothing | Guarantee must attach to a refund, and only in a landing legal section |
| "Guarantee" sits beside "Lab-verified HbA1c Tests" | Reads as a guaranteed *clinical* outcome |

`medical-safety` verdict: **blocked**, B1 hard fail.

### What's already good

"No Quinoa Bowls Required" is excellent — it names the competitor category's failure in four words without naming a competitor. Keep it.

---

## After

> **The Desi Diabetes Plate Guide**
> No Quinoa Bowls Required.
> **Work toward remission** and lower your HbA1c using everyday Pakistani kitchen foods.
>
> *Remission means an HbA1c below 6.5% held for at least three months without glucose-lowering medication. It's a documented outcome — not a promise, and not a cure.*
>
> […]
>
> **Ready to start your 90 days?**
> ✓ Lab-verified HbA1c tests ✓ Doctor-led care pod ✓ 100% desi kitchen friendly
>
> *Money-back guarantee on the program — full terms on the enrollment page.*

### What changed

| Change | Why |
|--------|-----|
| "Reverse" → "work toward remission" | Compliant on every channel; no exception needed |
| Added the definition | Also just better — a clinical guide should define its central term |
| "Guarantee Included" → "Money-back guarantee on the program" | Attaches to the refund, not the outcome; terms live on the landing page |
| Guarantee moved out of the benefits row | No longer reads as clinical |
| "Ready to Reverse Diabetes in 90 Days?" → "Ready to start your 90 days?" | Same energy, no claim |
| Kept "No Quinoa Bowls Required" | Best line in the asset |

## The lesson

The compliant version is **better marketing**, not weaker marketing.

"Reverse Type 2 Diabetes" is a claim any supplement seller can make, and Pakistani readers have heard it from people who were lying. "Remission means HbA1c below 6.5% for three months without medication — a documented outcome, not a promise" is a claim almost nobody else can make, and it's checkable.

The fix took about ten minutes. Most safety blocks do.
