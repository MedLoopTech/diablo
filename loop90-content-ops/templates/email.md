# Template — Email

★★★★★ Onboarding sequences and the weekly digest.

## Constraints

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Subject | 30–50 chars, no clickbait, no all-caps |
| Preview text | 40–90 chars, extends the subject — never repeats it |
| Body | 150–400 words |
| Reading level | Grade 6–8 (patient), 8–10 (prediabetic/family) |
| CTAs | One primary |
| Disclaimer | Full block on any medical content; footer otherwise |
| Required | Unsubscribe link |

## Structure

```
Subject
Preview text
—
Greeting (name where available)
Hook: 1–2 sentences, specific
Body: one idea, 2–3 short paragraphs
Takeaway: one action today
CTA button
Sign-off
Disclaimer + unsubscribe
```

## Variants

### Onboarding (day 0–7)

One concept per email. Never front-load the whole program.

| Day | Subject | Job |
|-----|---------|-----|
| 0 | Welcome to Loop/90 | Set expectations, name the care pod |
| 1 | Your first log takes 30 seconds | First action |
| 3 | Why we care about your fasting number | Educate |
| 5 | The 10-minute walk that changes dinner | Second habit |
| 7 | Week one — what your numbers say | Reflect, reinforce |

### Weekly digest (in-program)

```
Your week: [points] · [streak] days · day [n] of 90
What went well: [one specific thing]
One thing to try this week: [single action]
From your pod: [note if any]
```

Never lead with what they missed.

### Nurture (lead magnet → enrollment)

Value first. Sell in the last two emails, not the first.

| # | Job |
|---|-----|
| 1 | Deliver the guide, no ask |
| 2 | One useful idea from the guide, expanded |
| 3 | Story or mechanism — build belief |
| 4 | Introduce the program |
| 5 | Objections + enrollment CTA |

## Required elements

- [ ] Subject and preview text written and distinct
- [ ] One primary CTA
- [ ] One takeaway
- [ ] Disclaimer on medical content
- [ ] Unsubscribe link
- [ ] Desi examples if food appears

## Subject lines that work

- "Your fasting number, explained"
- "Roti isn't the problem. Portion is."
- "Week 3: what most people get wrong here"

## Subject lines to avoid

- "Reverse your diabetes NOW"
- "The secret your doctor won't tell you"
- Anything with a guaranteed number
- Fake urgency or fake personalisation ("re:", "fwd:")

## Never

- More than one primary CTA
- Medication content without routing to the doctor
- Shame for inactivity ("we noticed you've been slacking")
- Sending medical claims without the disclaimer block
