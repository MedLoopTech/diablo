# Template — Lead Magnet / Guide

The Desi Diabetes Plate Guide and its successors. Top-of-funnel asset for the prediabetic-family persona, and the sharable thing they hand to a parent.

## Constraints

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Length | 1500–3000 words |
| Reading level | Grade 8–10 **with glossary** |
| References | 3–5 |
| Disclaimer | Full block, on the final page |
| Format | PDF, mobile-readable, printable |

## Structure

```
Cover           — title, subtitle, Loop/90 mark
Who this is for — 3 sentences, name the reader
The idea        — the single concept in 1 page
The method      — the practical system (plate, portions, pairing)
Applied         — 8–12 real Pakistani meals worked through
Swaps table     — this → that, with why
Try this week   — 7 small actions, one per day
Glossary        — GI, GL, HbA1c, insulin resistance, remission
FAQ             — 5–8 real questions
References
Disclaimer + soft CTA
```

## Required elements

- [ ] One central idea — a guide with three ideas is three guides
- [ ] 8+ real Pakistani meals worked through with numbers
- [ ] Glossary for every technical term used
- [ ] Remission defined properly where first used
- [ ] 3–5 references with evidence levels
- [ ] Full disclaimer block
- [ ] Soft CTA only — this is value delivery, not a sales page
- [ ] Printable (works in black and white)
- [ ] Shareable without embarrassment — the reader may hand it to a parent

## The sharing test

This asset's highest-value job is being forwarded from an adult child to a diabetic parent. So:

- Nothing condescending
- Nothing that reads as an ad
- Nothing that implies the recipient failed
- Big enough type for a 60-year-old to read on a phone

## Swaps table format

| Instead of | Try | Why |
|------------|-----|-----|
| 2 plain parathas | 1 roti + extra daal | Lower load, more protein and fibre |
| White rice alone | Rice + kachumber + daal | Pairing blunts the spike |
| Sweet lassi | Salted lassi | Removes the added sugar, keeps the protein |

Always three columns. The "why" is what makes it stick.

## Soft CTA (final page)

> Want this applied to your own numbers, with a doctor, nutritionist, and coach who know your case? That's what our 90-day cohort does. [Learn more]

Never a countdown, never a discount, never scarcity on a lead magnet.

## Never

- Gating the value behind a signup wall mid-document
- Guaranteed outcomes
- Meal plans presented as medical prescriptions
- Western foods as the primary examples
