# Template — Instagram

★★★ Primary personas: prediabetic-family and nutritionist-coach. Visual-first.

## Constraints

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Caption | 125–300 words (first 125 chars visible before "more") |
| Carousel | 5–8 slides |
| Reading level | Grade 7–9 |
| Disclaimer | Final slide + caption line |
| Hashtags | 5–10, in the caption |

## Carousel structure

```
Slide 1 — Hook. Big text, one line. Must work as a thumbnail.
Slide 2 — The problem or the misconception
Slide 3–6 — The substance, one idea per slide
Slide 7 — Try this today (one action)
Slide 8 — Disclaimer + soft CTA
```

Design rules: one idea per slide, text large enough to read on a phone, real Pakistani food photography — never Western stock salad bowls.

## Caption structure

```
[Hook line — first 125 chars, must stand alone]

[2–3 short paragraphs expanding the carousel]

[One action]

[Soft CTA]

General information, not medical advice — talk to your doctor.

[5–10 hashtags]
```

## Post types that work

- Plate-method carousels with a real thali photo
- "Swap this for that" comparisons with desi foods
- Myth vs evidence (with the source named)
- GI/GL explained on one familiar food
- Behind-the-scenes of a care pod

## Required elements

- [ ] Slide 1 works as a standalone thumbnail
- [ ] One idea per slide
- [ ] Desi food imagery specified
- [ ] One action
- [ ] Disclaimer on final slide **and** in caption
- [ ] Sources named where a claim needs one

## Never

- Before/after body or HbA1c reveals
- Fear or shame framing
- "Reverse" or "guaranteed"
- Western stock food imagery as the default
- Claims dense enough to need a citation the format can't carry
