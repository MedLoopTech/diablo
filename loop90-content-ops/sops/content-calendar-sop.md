# SOP — Content Calendar

Monthly planning. Owned by `chief-content-strategist`, approved by the founder.

---

## Cadence

| When | What |
|------|------|
| Last week of the month | Plan the next month |
| Weekly | Check status of in-flight pieces |
| Quarterly | Review pillar coverage and what actually performed |

---

## Monthly planning, in order

### 1. Review last month

- What published, what stalled, and where it stalled
- Any piece that bounced 3+ times → the brief was wrong; note the pattern
- What scored ≥ 95 → gold candidates

### 2. Check pillar balance

Over the coming 10 pieces, target roughly:

| Tier | Share |
|------|-------|
| Tier 1 (Pillars 1–5) | 6–7 |
| Tier 2 (Pillars 6–9) | 2–3 |
| Tier 3 (Pillars 10–11) | 1–2 |

Never two consecutive pieces on the same pillar for the same persona.

### 3. Check funnel balance

Awareness content with no conversion content builds an audience you never ask. Conversion content with no awareness content has nobody to convert.

### 4. Check persona balance

Patient content dominates naturally — that's correct. But a month with zero doctor or coach content means recruitment stalls.

### 5. Apply seasonal hooks

| Period | Lead time |
|--------|-----------|
| Ramadan | 4 weeks (heaviest safety review) |
| Eid | 1 week |
| New Year | 3 weeks |
| World Diabetes Day (Nov 14) | 3 weeks |
| Summer | 2 weeks |

### 6. Pick anchors and map derivatives

One or two substantial anchors per month, each with its derivative map defined **at brief time** — this improves the anchor, not just the derivatives.

### 7. Leave slack

Reserve ~20% of capacity for rapid response. A fully booked calendar can't react.

## Weekly status check

Every in-flight piece has a `status`. Check for:

- Anything stuck at one status for over a week
- Anything blocked twice on the same issue → escalate, it's a scope problem
- Anything published without its memo archived → fix immediately
