# Repurposing Engine — Reference

## Derivation map

| Anchor | Yields well | Yields poorly |
|--------|-------------|---------------|
| Blog / SEO article | Video, carousel, WhatsApp, email | LinkedIn (unless professional angle) |
| Lead magnet | Carousel series, email sequence, blog excerpts | Short video (too dense) |
| Landing page | LinkedIn (B2B version), email | Patient WhatsApp |
| Doctor pitch | LinkedIn, professional email | Anything patient-facing |
| Case study | Video, carousel, LinkedIn | WhatsApp (needs consent handling) |

## Disclaimer form by channel — never inherited

| Channel | Required form |
|---------|---------------|
| Blog | Full block at end |
| Landing | Footer + near health claims |
| WhatsApp | Link to disclaimer page |
| In-app announcement | None inline, but zero banned terms |
| Short video | **Spoken AND on-screen** |
| Instagram | Final slide + caption line |
| Email | Full block on medical content |
| LinkedIn (doctor) | "Clinical decisions remain yours" |

This table is why clearance never transfers.

## Compression ratios

| From | To | Realistic |
|------|-----|-----------|
| 1400-word blog | Short video | ~120 spoken words |
| 1400-word blog | Carousel | 6–8 slides, ~25 words each |
| 1400-word blog | WhatsApp | 60–100 words |
| 1400-word blog | Email | 250–350 words |

At these ratios you are **choosing one idea**, not compressing five. Decide which idea before writing.

## When a variant shouldn't exist

Skip and say why:

- No visual contrast → no short video
- No professional-audience angle → no LinkedIn
- Topic needs citation density the format can't carry → no Instagram
- Anchor is medication-adjacent → no short video (no room for the routing nuance)

Skipping is a valid output. Manufacturing a weak variant to fill a slot is not.

## The new-claim trap

The most common failure: a slide or a script beat feels thin, so a statistic appears to strengthen it. That statistic came from training data, not the memo.

Check every variant: is there a number, percentage, or comparative claim that isn't in the anchor? If yes, cut it or flag it as a research need.

## Persona shift rules

| Shift | Handling |
|-------|----------|
| Same persona, new channel | Straightforward repurpose |
| patient → prediabetic-family | Load the new persona; raise reading level; add mechanism |
| patient → doctor | **New brief.** Different evidence bar, different register, different objections |
| Any → pharma | **New brief.** Always. |

If you find yourself rewriting rather than deriving, it's a new brief.

## Sequencing

Publish the anchor first, then variants over the following days — each variant links back, so the anchor accumulates traffic and authority. Publishing a Reel before its blog wastes the link.
