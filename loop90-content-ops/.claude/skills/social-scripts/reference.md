# Social Scripts — Reference

## Short-video beat structure (60s)

| Beat | Seconds | Job |
|------|---------|-----|
| Hook | 0–3 | Stop the scroll. Visual or contrast, not a claim. |
| Setup | 3–15 | Name the situation they recognise |
| Payoff | 15–40 | The one idea, with the mechanism in a sentence |
| Action | 40–52 | One thing to do today |
| Disclaimer | 52–60 | Spoken + on screen |

**Eight seconds for the disclaimer is not optional.** Budget it first, then write the content into what's left.

## Carousel structure (7 slides)

| Slide | Job |
|-------|-----|
| 1 | Hook — works as a thumbnail, big text, one line |
| 2 | The misconception or the problem |
| 3–5 | The substance, one idea per slide |
| 6 | Try this today |
| 7 | Disclaimer + soft CTA |

## LinkedIn structure

```
Line 1-2   Hook (all most people see)
Line 3-4   The problem in their language
Body       3-5 short paragraphs or a tight list
Close      One question or one low-friction CTA
```

## Word budgets

| Format | Budget |
|--------|--------|
| 60s video voiceover | 120–150 words |
| 45s video voiceover | 90–110 words |
| Carousel slide | 20–30 words |
| Carousel caption | 125–300 words |
| LinkedIn post | 150–400 words |

## Voiceover vs on-screen

They should do different jobs:

| Voiceover | On screen |
|-----------|-----------|
| "The rice hasn't changed — the plate has" | *Same rice. Different plate.* |
| "Protein and fibre slow everything down" | *Daal + kachumber = slower rise* |

Duplicating the voiceover on screen wastes both.

## Code-switching in video

Roman Urdu works well in patient short-form — it's how people speak. But:

- Thresholds and safety lines stay in clear English or unambiguous Urdu
- The spoken disclaimer can be Urdu; the on-screen one should be English (screenshots get shared)
- Never code-switch in doctor or pharma content

## Hashtags

3–5 for LinkedIn, 5–10 for Instagram. Relevant, not volume-chasing.

Never hashtag a banned term. `#reversediabetes` is a banned term in a hashtag.

## Format-fit test

Before scripting, ask:

1. Is there one idea, not two?
2. Is there a visual or a contrast to hook on?
3. Can the honest version — with every required caveat — fit the budget?

Two yeses and a no on (3) means the answer is a blog post.
