# Chief Content Strategist — Reference

## Persona → channel fit

| Persona | Best channels | Avoid |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Patient | WhatsApp, in-app, email, short video | LinkedIn |
| Prediabetic / family | Blog/SEO, lead magnet, Instagram, email | In-app (they're not enrolled) |
| Doctor | LinkedIn, professional landing, direct pitch | Instagram, patient WhatsApp |
| Nutritionist / coach | Instagram, LinkedIn, WhatsApp | Patient-facing anything |
| Pharma | Direct pitch, LinkedIn, executive email | Consumer channels entirely |

## Funnel stage → content type

| Stage | Job | Typical channels |
|-------|-----|------------------|
| Awareness | Answer a question they're already asking | Blog/SEO, Instagram, short video |
| Nurture | Build belief in the mechanism | Email, lead magnet, blog |
| Conversion | Address objections, make the ask | Landing page, email 4–5, pitch |
| Retention | Keep them logging and hopeful | In-app, WhatsApp nudge, weekly digest |

## Angle selection

Strong angles come from tension, not topics.

| Weak angle | Strong angle |
|------------|--------------|
| "Benefits of fibre" | "Why daal chawal spikes you less than plain rice" |
| "Exercise is good for diabetes" | "The 10 minutes after dinner that change your morning number" |
| "About our program" | "Your doctor sees you twice a year. Who's there the other 363 days?" |
| "Ramadan and diabetes" | "Fasting when you're on insulin: what to ask your doctor first" |

Test: could a competitor publish this headline unchanged? If yes, it's not an angle.

## Pillar balance

Over any 10 pieces, aim roughly for:

| Tier | Share |
|------|-------|
| Tier 1 (Pillars 1–5) | 6–7 pieces |
| Tier 2 (Pillars 6–9) | 2–3 pieces |
| Tier 3 (Pillars 10–11) | 1–2 pieces |

Never publish two consecutive pieces on the same pillar for the same persona.

## Seasonal calendar

| Period | Hook | Lead time |
|--------|------|-----------|
| Ramadan | Fasting safety, iftar plate, suhoor protein | 4 weeks before |
| Eid | Sweets strategy, portion, post-meal walks | 1 week before |
| New Year | Cohort enrollment, fresh start | 3 weeks before |
| World Diabetes Day (Nov 14) | Remission awareness, free guide | 3 weeks before |
| Summer | Hydration, lassi swaps, heat and glucose | 2 weeks before |

Ramadan content needs the longest lead because it carries the heaviest safety review.

## When to say no to a request

- Topic maps to no pillar → ask the user whether the pillar list should change; don't smuggle it in
- Persona doesn't exist → don't invent one
- The claim has no evidence at any level → the piece can't be written honestly
- The request needs a banned term on a channel that forbids it → offer the compliant alternative

## Brief quality test

Before handing off, check:

- [ ] Could `writing` draft this without asking a question?
- [ ] Is `single_takeaway` one sentence?
- [ ] Are objections pulled from the actual persona file, not invented?
- [ ] Are safety flags set?
- [ ] Does `do_not_include` name the obvious scope creep?

If any answer is no, the brief isn't done.
